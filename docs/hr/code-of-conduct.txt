1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
-------------------
Acme Corporation is committed to maintaining a workplace characterized by
integrity, respect, transparency, and professionalism. This Code of Conduct
applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, interns, and anyone acting
on behalf of Acme Corporation, regardless of location, role, or seniority.
Adherence to this Code is a condition of employment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR & WORKPLACE STANDARDS
------------------------------------------------
   - Employees are expected to conduct themselves in a professional,
     courteous, and respectful manner at all times — with colleagues,
     clients, vendors, and the public.
   - Punctuality, reliability, and accountability are core professional
     expectations.
   - Constructive collaboration, open communication, and respect for
     differing perspectives are encouraged.
   - Employees must not engage in gossip, spreading rumors, or any
     behavior that undermines team morale or trust.
   - Workplace disputes must be escalated through appropriate channels
     (manager, HR) — not through social media or informal channels.
   - Employees are expected to maintain a clean, organized, and safe
     working environment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. WORKING HOURS
-----------------
   - Core Hours: All employees are expected to be available and working
     between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM (Monday through Friday).
   - Flexible Hours: Outside of core hours, employees have flexibility to
     structure their remaining working hours based on role requirements and
     manager agreement. Total expected working hours remain 9 hours per day
     (including a 1-hour break).
   - Overtime must be pre-approved by the direct manager and is compensated
     as per the company's overtime policy.
   - Work-from-home arrangements must be agreed upon in writing with the
     manager and HR.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. DRESS CODE
--------------
   - Weekdays (Monday through Thursday): Business Casual attire is required.
     Business casual includes collared shirts, trousers/formal pants,
     blouses, skirts of appropriate length, and closed-toe shoes.
   - Casual Fridays: Employees may dress casually on Fridays. Acceptable
     casual wear includes jeans (without tears/rips), polo shirts, and
     clean sneakers. Shorts, flip-flops, and loungewear are not permitted
     even on Casual Fridays.
   - Client-facing days: Business formal or as specified by the department head.
   - Clothing with offensive graphics, slogans, or branding that conflicts
     with company values is never acceptable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. ANTI-HARASSMENT & NON-DISCRIMINATION POLICY
------------------------------------------------
   - Acme Corporation maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment,
     bullying, discrimination, or intimidation in any form.
   - Harassment includes: verbal abuse, offensive jokes, unwanted physical
     contact, sexual harassment, threatening behavior, and cyberbullying.
   - Discrimination based on gender, age, religion, caste, race, disability,
     sexual orientation, nationality, or marital status is strictly prohibited.
   - Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment must report it
     to HR at hr@company.com or via the anonymous ethics hotline.
   - All complaints will be investigated promptly, confidentially, and
     impartially. Retaliation against a complainant is itself a serious
     violation of this policy.
   - The company constitutes an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) per the
     POSH Act, 2013, for handling sexual harassment complaints.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION
--------------------------------------
   - Employees have access to confidential information including trade secrets,
     client data, financial data, employee records, and product roadmaps.
   - All such information must be treated as strictly confidential and must
     not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals inside or outside the company.
   - Confidentiality obligations extend beyond the term of employment.
     Employees remain bound by NDAs signed at onboarding even after separation.
   - Employees must not use confidential company information for personal gain.
   - Company data must be stored only on approved systems; use of personal
     cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) for company data is prohibited.
   - Any data breach or suspected leak must be reported to the IT Security
     team immediately at security@company.com.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. SOCIAL MEDIA GUIDELINES
----------------------------
   - Employees may use personal social media but must not post confidential
     company information, internal communications, financial data, or
     any content that could disparage the company, its clients, or colleagues.
   - When identifying oneself as an employee of Acme Corporation online,
     posts must include a disclaimer that views are personal, not the company's.
   - Do not share unreleased products, earnings data, litigation matters,
     or internal strategic plans on any social media platform.
   - Engaging in public debates or confrontational interactions that could
     embarrass the company is prohibited.
   - Monitoring of publicly visible social media may occur as part of the
     company's reputation management program.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
------------------------
   - Employees must disclose any situation where personal interests could
     conflict — or appear to conflict — with the interests of the company.
   - Examples include: owning equity in a competitor, having a relative
     employed by a key vendor, or receiving personal benefits from suppliers.
   - Conflict of interest disclosures must be submitted to HR and Legal
     using the COI Disclosure Form on the HR portal within 7 days of
     the conflict arising.
   - Failure to disclose a known conflict of interest is a disciplinary offense.
   - Employees involved in vendor selection, procurement, or contracting must
     declare all associations with potential vendors.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. GIFTS & ENTERTAINMENT POLICY
---------------------------------
   - Accepting gifts from clients, vendors, or external parties is permitted
     only within defined limits.
   - Gifts with a total value EXCEEDING Rs 2,000 (two thousand) MUST be
     declared to the employee's manager and the Compliance team within
     5 business days of receipt.
   - Non-declared gifts above Rs 2,000 must be returned or surrendered to
     the company.
   - Cash gifts, gift cards equivalent to cash, and gifts from parties
     involved in pending procurement decisions are NEVER acceptable.
   - Business entertainment (meals, events) with clients is permitted when
     reasonable and recorded on the expense report.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION
------------------------------
   - Acme Corporation encourages employees to report unethical behavior,
     fraud, safety violations, or policy breaches without fear of retaliation.
   - Reports can be made to: ethics@company.com or via the anonymous
     whistleblower hotline: 1800-XXX-XXXX.
   - The identity of the whistleblower will be kept confidential to the
     maximum extent possible.
   - Retaliation against a whistleblower — including demotion, termination,
     harassment, or adverse treatment — is a serious violation and will
     itself be investigated and penalized.
   - False reports made in bad faith may result in disciplinary action
     against the reporting party.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS
--------------------------
   Violations of this Code of Conduct will be addressed through a structured
   disciplinary process:

   Step 1 — VERBAL WARNING:
      For minor first-time violations. The manager formally documents
      the discussion. Employee is counseled on expected behavior.

   Step 2 — WRITTEN WARNING:
      For repeated minor violations or a moderate first-time violation.
      Issued in writing, placed in the employee's personnel file.
      Improvement expectations and timelines are defined.

   Step 3 — SUSPENSION:
      For serious violations or repeated misconduct after written warning.
      Employee may be suspended without pay for 1–15 days pending
      investigation or as a disciplinary measure.

   Step 4 — TERMINATION:
      For gross misconduct, fraud, harassment, data theft, or failure to
      improve after prior disciplinary steps. Employment is ended immediately.
      Termination for cause may impact settlement benefits.

   Note: Depending on the severity, the company reserves the right to
   skip earlier steps and proceed directly to suspension or termination.
