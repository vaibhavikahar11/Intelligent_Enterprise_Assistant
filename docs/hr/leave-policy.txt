1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy outlines the types of leave available to employees, the entitlements,
procedures for applying, and rules governing leave management at Acme Corporation.
This policy applies to all full-time employees unless otherwise stated in their
employment contract.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. TYPES OF LEAVE AND ENTITLEMENTS
-----------------------------------

2.1 SICK LEAVE
   - Entitlement: 12 (twelve) days per calendar year.
   - Intended for personal illness, medical appointments, or recovery.
   - A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for
     absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
   - CARRY-FORWARD: Sick leaves CANNOT be carried forward to the next year.
     Any unused sick leave at year-end is FORFEITED and will NOT be credited
     to the following year under any circumstances.
   - Sick leave CANNOT be encashed.

2.2 CASUAL LEAVE
   - Entitlement: 18 (eighteen) days per calendar year.
   - Intended for personal errands, family obligations, or short personal needs.
   - Casual leave should ideally not exceed 3 consecutive days; longer
     absences should be covered under Annual Leave.
   - CARRY-FORWARD: Casual leaves CANNOT be carried forward to the next year.
     Unused casual leave at year-end is FORFEITED automatically.
   - Casual leave CANNOT be encashed.

2.3 ANNUAL LEAVE (EARNED LEAVE)
   - Entitlement: 21 (twenty-one) days per calendar year.
   - Accrues at a rate of 1.75 days per completed month of service.
   - Annual leave is intended for rest, vacation, and personal planning.
   - Minimum duration: 1 day; can be combined with public holidays.
   - CARRY-FORWARD: Annual leave CAN be carried forward to the next year,
     up to a maximum of 15 (fifteen) days. Any balance exceeding 15 days
     at year-end is automatically forfeited unless approved encashment is
     applied.
   - Annual leave CAN be encashed (refer to Section 6).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. SPECIAL LEAVE CATEGORIES
-----------------------------

3.1 MATERNITY LEAVE
   - Entitlement: 26 (twenty-six) weeks of paid maternity leave as per the
     Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017.
   - Applicable to female employees with at least 80 days of service in the
     12 months preceding the expected delivery date.
   - Can commence up to 8 weeks prior to the expected date of delivery.
   - Applicable for up to 2 surviving children.
   - Adoption and surrogacy provisions apply as per statutory guidelines.

3.2 PATERNITY LEAVE
   - Entitlement: 5 (five) days of paid paternity leave.
   - Must be availed within 30 days of the birth of the child.
   - Applicable to male employees with at least 1 year of service.

3.3 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
   - Entitlement: 3 (three) days of paid bereavement leave for the death
     of an immediate family member.
   - Immediate family includes: spouse, parent, parent-in-law, sibling,
     child, or legal guardian.
   - Additional days may be granted at the discretion of the department head.
   - Documentary evidence (death certificate) may be required.

3.4 LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
   - Leave Without Pay may be granted when all applicable paid leave is
     exhausted and a genuine need exists.
   - LWP REQUIRES explicit written approval from the Vice President (VP)
     of the respective department.
   - LWP may impact annual appraisals, bonuses, and increment eligibility
     depending on its duration.
   - Extended LWP of more than 30 days requires HR Director review.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------

4.1 PLANNED LEAVE
   - All planned leave applications MUST be submitted a minimum of 3 (three)
     days in advance through the HR portal at: https://hr.company.com
   - Employees must select the appropriate leave type, specify dates, and
     provide a brief reason.
   - Leave is subject to manager approval; approval must be received before
     the employee proceeds on leave.

4.2 EMERGENCY / UNPLANNED LEAVE
   - In the event of an emergency, the employee must notify their direct
     manager within 24 hours of the absence.
   - Subsequent formal leave application must be filed on the HR portal
     at https://hr.company.com on the employee's return.
   - Failure to notify within 24 hours may result in the absence being
     marked as Leave Without Pay.

4.3 HR PORTAL
   - All leave requests, approvals, and balances are managed exclusively
     through the HR portal: https://hr.company.com
   - Employees are responsible for maintaining accurate leave records.
   - Leave balance can be checked in real time via the portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. LEAVE APPROVAL HIERARCHY
-----------------------------
   Level 1: Direct Manager / Team Lead (up to 5 days)
   Level 2: Department Head (6 to 15 days)
   Level 3: HR Director + VP (LWP or leaves exceeding 15 days)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
---------------------
   - Only Annual (Earned) Leave can be encashed.
   - Sick Leave and Casual Leave CANNOT be encashed under any circumstances.
   - Encashment is calculated at the employee's current basic salary rate.
   - Formula: (Basic Monthly Salary / 26) × Number of Leave Days Encashed.
   - Maximum encashment per year is limited to 15 days (the carry-forward cap).
   - Leave encashment is subject to applicable taxes as per Indian tax law.
   - Encashment requests must be submitted via the HR portal before December 15
     of each calendar year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
-------------------
   - Employees are entitled to all national public holidays as declared by the
     government.
   - The HR department publishes the official company holiday list at the
     beginning of each calendar year via the HR portal.
   - Working on a public holiday requires prior approval and will be compensated
     as per the company overtime policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. LEAVE LAPSING & YEAR-END PROCESSING
----------------------------------------
   - The leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.
   - Sick Leave balance: LAPSES COMPLETELY on December 31. No carry-forward.
   - Casual Leave balance: LAPSES COMPLETELY on December 31. No carry-forward.
   - Annual Leave balance: Up to 15 days carried forward; excess is forfeited.
   - Year-end leave processing is automated via the HR portal on January 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. POLICY VIOLATIONS
---------------------
   - Misrepresentation of leave reasons may result in disciplinary action.
   - Unauthorized absence beyond 5 days without notification may lead to
     termination of employment.
   - Leave abuse patterns will be addressed through the performance review process.
