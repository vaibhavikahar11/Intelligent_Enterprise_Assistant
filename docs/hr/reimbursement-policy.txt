1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy establishes guidelines for reimbursing employees for legitimate
business expenses incurred in the course of performing their duties for
Acme Corporation. The intent is to ensure employees are fairly compensated
for authorized expenses while maintaining fiscal responsibility and compliance.
This policy applies to all permanent employees and consultants on approved
engagement letters.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT
------------------------

2.1 DOMESTIC TRAVEL (Within India)
   - Air Travel: Economy class tickets only for all domestic travel.
     Business class for domestic travel is NOT eligible for reimbursement.
   - Train Travel: AC First Class or AC 2-Tier permissible.
   - Road Travel: Company cab or personal vehicle (see Mileage Reimbursement).
   - Booking: All domestic travel must be booked through the approved travel
     desk or via the company travel portal at travel.company.com.
   - Advance booking (at least 7 days prior) is encouraged to minimize costs.

2.2 INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL
   - Air Travel: Economy class for flights UNDER 8 hours duration.
   - Air Travel: Business class is permitted for international flights with
     a total journey duration EXCEEDING 8 hours (layovers included).
   - All international travel requires written approval from the department
     VP before ticket booking.
   - Visa fees, travel insurance, and airport transfers are reimbursable
     with supporting documentation.

2.3 MILEAGE REIMBURSEMENT
   - Employees using their personal vehicle for official duty are reimbursed
     at Rs 12 (twelve) per kilometer.
   - Route and distance must be declared on the reimbursement form.
   - GPS-based route verification may be applied for distances above 100 km.
   - Fuel receipts should be retained and attached; however, mileage
     reimbursement is a flat rate — actual fuel bills are not reimbursed
     separately in addition to the per-km rate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. MEAL ALLOWANCE
------------------
   - Domestic Travel: Rs 500 (five hundred) per day per employee.
     This covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined.
   - International Travel: $30 USD per day (or local currency equivalent).
   - Meal allowance is not applicable for day trips within the city of
     the employee's base location.
   - Meals hosted for clients are claimed under "Client Entertainment"
     (see Section 8) and are separate from the daily meal allowance.
   - Alcohol is NOT covered under any meal or entertainment reimbursement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. ACCOMMODATION
-----------------

4.1 DOMESTIC ACCOMMODATION
   - Maximum hotel reimbursement: Rs 8,000 (eight thousand) per night
     for domestic travel.
   - In metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata),
     a 20% premium (up to Rs 9,600/night) may be approved by the department head.
   - Hotels must be booked via the company travel portal where possible.
   - Lodging with family or friends: a fixed allowance of Rs 1,500/night applies.

4.2 INTERNATIONAL ACCOMMODATION
   - Maximum hotel reimbursement: $200 USD (two hundred US dollars) per night.
   - This rate is a cap; employees are encouraged to book within budget.
   - Hotel must be in a safe, business-appropriate location.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. SUBMISSION DEADLINE — CRITICAL RULE
----------------------------------------
   >> ALL REIMBURSEMENT CLAIMS MUST BE SUBMITTED WITHIN 30 (THIRTY) DAYS
      OF THE DATE THE EXPENSE WAS INCURRED. <<

   - Claims submitted AFTER 30 days from the expense date will NOT be
     processed and will be REJECTED without exception.
   - No retrospective approval or exception will be granted for late submissions
     regardless of reason, unless approved in writing by the CFO.
   - Employees are strongly advised to submit claims promptly and not wait
     until the month-end to avoid missing the 30-day deadline.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. RECEIPT & DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------------------------
   - ALL receipts/invoices for expenses exceeding Rs 500 MUST be attached
     to the reimbursement claim.
   - Receipts below Rs 500 are recommended but not mandatory.
   - Receipts must clearly show: vendor name, date, amount, GST number (if applicable).
   - Digital/scanned copies of receipts are accepted via the reimbursement portal.
   - Missing receipts for amounts above Rs 500 will result in automatic rejection
     of that line item.
   - Credit card statements alone are NOT acceptable as receipts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. MEDICAL REIMBURSEMENT
--------------------------
   - Eligible employees can claim up to Rs 15,000 (fifteen thousand) per
     financial year (April to March) for medical expenses.
   - Covers: prescription medicines, diagnostic tests, hospitalization
     (not covered by group insurance), dental, and vision care.
   - Medical bills must be in the employee's name or that of their declared
     dependent (spouse, children, parents).
   - Group health insurance claims are separate and processed via the
     insurance portal — do not submit insurance-covered expenses here.
   - Any unused medical reimbursement limit does NOT carry forward to
     the next financial year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. MOBILE BILL REIMBURSEMENT
------------------------------
   - Monthly Limit: Rs 1,500 (one thousand five hundred) per month for
     employees in eligible grades/designations.
   - Eligible roles are defined by the HR grade matrix (available on the
     HR portal).
   - Original postpaid bill or prepaid top-up receipt must be submitted.
   - Reimbursement covers official usage; the full bill is reimbursed
     up to the cap regardless of personal vs. official usage distinction.
   - Prepaid recharges are reimbursable with a valid receipt.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. INTERNET ALLOWANCE (WORK-FROM-HOME)
----------------------------------------
   - Monthly Limit: Rs 1,000 (one thousand) per month for employees
     officially designated as Work-From-Home (WFH) or Hybrid.
   - Broadband/fiber internet bill must be submitted as evidence.
   - Mobile data plans claimed under the Internet Allowance cannot
     simultaneously be claimed under Mobile Bill Reimbursement.
   - Allowance is not payable for months when the employee is on extended
     leave (more than 15 days in a month).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. APPROVAL & PAYMENT PROCESS
--------------------------------
   Step 1: Employee completes the Reimbursement Request Form on the portal.
   Step 2: All supporting receipts are uploaded digitally.
   Step 3: The Reimbursement Form must be APPROVED BY THE DIRECT MANAGER.
           The manager is expected to review and approve within 5 working days.
   Step 4: Approved claims are forwarded to the Finance team for verification.
   Step 5: Payment is processed within 15 (fifteen) WORKING DAYS of
           manager approval.
   Step 6: Reimbursement is credited to the employee's registered bank account
           (same as salary account).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11. POLICY VIOLATIONS & FRAUDULENT CLAIMS
-------------------------------------------
   - Submitting false or inflated claims constitutes fraud and will result in
     immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
   - The Finance team conducts random audits of submitted claims.
   - Employees found to have violated this policy will be required to refund
     all improperly claimed amounts with interest.
