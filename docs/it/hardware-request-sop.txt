                    ACME CORPORATION - IT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
                              HARDWARE REQUEST & ASSET MANAGEMENT
                    Document Version: 1.6 | Effective Date: 01-Jan-2024
                         Maintained by: IT Operations & Asset Management Team

1. PURPOSE
----------
This SOP defines the process for requesting, receiving, managing, and returning
IT hardware assets at Acme Corporation. It applies to all employees requiring
standard or specialized computing equipment, and establishes accountability for
company-owned hardware throughout the asset lifecycle.

All hardware assigned to employees remains the property of Acme Corporation
and must be handled responsibly, returned in good condition, and accounted for
at all times.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. HARDWARE REQUEST PORTAL
----------------------------
   - All hardware requests must be submitted via the IT Hardware Request Portal:
     URL: https://it-requests.company.com
   - Log in with your corporate credentials.
   - Select "New Hardware Request" and fill in all required fields.
   - Hardware requests submitted via email or verbal communication will
     NOT be processed without a corresponding portal ticket.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. STANDARD EQUIPMENT LIST
----------------------------
   The following constitute standard equipment that employees may request
   based on role eligibility:

   a) Laptop (Windows or macOS based on role requirement)
   b) Docking Station (for office desk setups)
   c) External Monitor (24" standard; 27" for design/development roles)
   d) Keyboard (wired or wireless)
   e) Mouse (wired or wireless)
   f) Headset (for contact center or designated remote employees)
   g) Laptop bag / backpack (for field/remote employees)

   Non-standard items (e.g., dual monitors, ergonomic chairs, drawing tablets,
   specialized peripherals) require additional justification and approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. LAPTOP REFRESH CYCLE
------------------------
   - Standard Laptop Refresh Cycle: Every 3 (three) years of service.
   - Laptops in service for 3 or more years are eligible for hardware refresh.
   - The IT Asset Management team will proactively contact employees
     approaching their refresh eligibility date.
   - Early refresh requests (before 3 years) require documented justification
     (e.g., severe performance degradation, hardware failure) and must be
     approved by the department head and IT Director.
   - Old hardware is collected by IT during the refresh and is either
     refurbished, donated, or disposed of in accordance with the company's
     e-waste policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. APPROVAL PROCESS
--------------------
   Step 1: Employee submits hardware request at https://it-requests.company.com.
   Step 2: Automatic notification is sent to the employee's DIRECT MANAGER
           for approval. Manager approval is MANDATORY.
   Step 3: Manager reviews and approves or rejects the request within
           3 business days. Rejected requests include a reason.
   Step 4: Approved requests are forwarded to the IT Asset Management team
           for processing.
   Step 5: IT confirms availability from stock or initiates procurement.
   Step 6: Employee is notified with expected delivery date.
   Step 7: Equipment is delivered; employee signs the Asset Acknowledgment Form.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. DELIVERY TIMELINES
-----------------------
   - Standard Equipment (from standard equipment list):
     Delivery within 5 to 7 (five to seven) BUSINESS DAYS from manager approval.

   - Custom / Specialized Equipment (non-standard items, special configurations,
     or items requiring procurement from vendors):
     Delivery within UP TO 3 (three) WEEKS from manager approval.

   - Urgent requests must be flagged at the time of submission with a business
     justification. IT will make reasonable efforts to expedite; however,
     guaranteed SLAs do not apply to urgent requests.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. EQUIPMENT ISSUANCE & ASSET TRACKING
----------------------------------------
   - All hardware is tagged with a unique Asset ID barcode upon issuance.
   - The employee must sign an Asset Acknowledgment Form confirming:
     a) Receipt of the equipment in good working condition.
     b) Understanding that the equipment is company property.
     c) Agreement to return the equipment upon separation or request.
   - Asset records are maintained in the IT Asset Management System.
   - Employees must report any change in equipment location (e.g., permanent
     WFH, office transfer) to the IT team within 5 business days.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. EQUIPMENT CARE & RESPONSIBILITY
------------------------------------
   - Equipment is company property and must be used responsibly.
   - Employees are responsible for:
     a) Physical safety of the equipment in their custody.
     b) Not lending equipment to family members or unauthorized users.
     c) Keeping equipment updated with company-mandated OS and security patches.
     d) Reporting any hardware malfunction promptly via the IT portal.
   - Equipment must not be modified, disassembled, or used to install
     unauthorized operating systems.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. DAMAGE, LOSS & THEFT POLICY
--------------------------------

   9.1 ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE
      - FIRST INCIDENT: A single accidental damage incident (e.g., accidental
        drop, liquid spill) is covered by the company at no cost to the employee,
        provided the incident is promptly reported.
      - SUBSEQUENT INCIDENTS: Second and subsequent accidental damage incidents
        in the same equipment lifecycle may result in partial or full cost recovery
        from the employee, at management discretion.
      - Intentional damage is never covered and constitutes a disciplinary offense.

   9.2 LOST EQUIPMENT
      - Lost or stolen equipment MUST be reported IMMEDIATELY to:
        a) IT Helpdesk: Ext. 1234 | it-support@company.com
        b) Corporate Security: security@company.com
      - A formal incident report must be filed within 24 hours of discovery.
      - IT will immediately remotely wipe the device (if company-managed) to
        protect company data.
      - Theft must also be reported to local law enforcement and an FIR copy
        submitted to HR and IT.
      - Loss due to negligence may result in cost recovery from the employee
        as assessed by the IT Director and HR.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION REQUESTS
------------------------------------
   - Requests for software installation are SEPARATE from hardware requests.
   - Submit software installation requests via: https://helpdesk.company.com
     Select category: "Software Installation Request."
   - Standard software is available via the Self-Service Software Portal
     at https://software.company.com and can be installed without a ticket.
   - Non-standard or licensed software requires manager approval and
     IT security review before installation.
   - Employees must NOT install unauthorized, pirated, or personal software
     on company hardware.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11. EQUIPMENT RETURN ON SEPARATION
------------------------------------
   - ALL company hardware must be returned to IT on or before the last
     working day of employment.
   - IT will coordinate equipment collection; employees must ensure all
     equipment (laptop, docking station, peripherals, accessories, and bags)
     is returned in working condition.
   - Final clearance (required for full & final settlement) will not be
     issued until all IT assets are returned and cleared.
   - Employees must remove all personal data and sign out of all personal
     accounts before returning equipment; IT will perform a full wipe post-return.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
