1. PURPOSE
----------
This SOP defines the procedures for password creation, management, reset, and
account lockout resolution for all Acme Corporation systems. Following these
procedures ensures the security of corporate accounts and protects company data
from unauthorized access.

This SOP applies to all employees, contractors, and external users who have
been granted access to Acme Corporation's systems and applications.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. PASSWORD POLICY REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------
All corporate system passwords MUST conform to the following requirements:

   2.1 MINIMUM LENGTH
      - Passwords must be a minimum of 12 (twelve) characters long.
      - Longer passphrases (16+ characters) are strongly encouraged.

   2.2 COMPLEXITY REQUIREMENTS
      All passwords must contain ALL FOUR of the following character types:
      a) At least one UPPERCASE letter (A–Z)
      b) At least one LOWERCASE letter (a–z)
      c) At least one NUMBER (0–9)
      d) At least one SPECIAL CHARACTER (e.g., @, #, $, %, !, *, &, ^)

   2.3 PASSWORD REUSE
      - Employees CANNOT reuse any of their last 10 (ten) previously
        used passwords.
      - The system will automatically reject any password that matches
        any of the 10 most recent passwords for that account.

   2.4 PASSWORD EXPIRY
      - Passwords expire every 90 (ninety) days.
      - Users will receive reminder notifications 14 days, 7 days, and
        1 day before expiry.
      - After expiry, users will be forced to change their password on
        next login before accessing any corporate systems.
      - Accounts with expired passwords may lose access to critical
        systems; do not wait until the last day.

   2.5 PROHIBITED PASSWORDS
      - Do not use: your name, username, company name, sequential
        numbers (123456), keyboard patterns (qwerty), or dictionary words.
      - Common passwords identified in breach databases are automatically
        blocked by the system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT POLICY
---------------------------
   - After 5 (five) consecutive failed login attempts, the account will
     be AUTOMATICALLY LOCKED for 30 (thirty) minutes.
   - After the 30-minute lockout period, the account is automatically
     unlocked and the user may attempt login again.
   - If the user cannot wait 30 minutes (e.g., urgent business need),
     they must contact the IT Helpdesk for manual unlock (see Section 5).
   - Repeated lockouts within a short period will trigger a security
     alert and may require identity verification before unlock.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. SELF-SERVICE PASSWORD RESET
--------------------------------
   - Employees can reset their own passwords 24x7 using the Self-Service
     Identity Portal at: https://identity.company.com
   - To use the self-service portal, the employee must have previously:
     a) Registered their mobile number for OTP verification.
     b) Set up their security questions (minimum 3 questions).
     c) Enrolled in Microsoft Authenticator MFA (see Section 6).

   SELF-SERVICE RESET STEPS:
   Step 1: Visit https://identity.company.com
   Step 2: Click "Forgot Password / Reset Password."
   Step 3: Enter your corporate email address (username@company.com).
   Step 4: Choose a verification method:
           - OTP sent to registered mobile number, OR
           - Approval via Microsoft Authenticator app.
   Step 5: Answer the security question if prompted.
   Step 6: Enter and confirm your new password (meeting all requirements
           in Section 2).
   Step 7: Click "Reset Password." The new password is effective immediately.
   Step 8: Log in to all corporate applications using the new password.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. HELPDESK-ASSISTED RESET
----------------------------
   If self-service reset is unavailable or the account is locked out:

   - Call IT Helpdesk: Internal Extension 1234
     (Dial 1234 from any internal phone or via the softphone)
   - Email IT Support: it-support@company.com
     (Note: email may be inaccessible if your account is locked — use phone)

   The IT Helpdesk agent will:
   a) Verify the caller's identity using employee ID and security questions.
   b) Unlock the account and/or trigger a password reset link.
   c) Guide the user through the reset process if needed.
   d) Log the incident in the IT ticketing system for audit purposes.

   Helpdesk Operating Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST.

6. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
--------------------------------------
   MFA is mandatory for all corporate accounts and remote access.

   SETTING UP MFA — MICROSOFT AUTHENTICATOR:
   Step 1: Download the Microsoft Authenticator app:
           - iOS: Search "Microsoft Authenticator" on the App Store.
           - Android: Search "Microsoft Authenticator" on the Play Store.
   Step 2: Open the app and tap "Add Account."
   Step 3: Select "Work or school account."
   Step 4: Scan the QR code displayed at https://identity.company.com/mfa
           or enter the setup code manually.
   Step 5: The app will generate a 6-digit time-based OTP — enter it on
           the portal to confirm setup.
   Step 6: Mark this device as "trusted" if it is a company-managed device.

   Once MFA is configured:
   - Every login will prompt for the MFA code or push notification approval.
   - Approving MFA push notifications from unknown login attempts must be
     DENIED immediately and reported to security@company.com.

8. BEST PRACTICES & SECURITY REMINDERS
----------------------------------------
   - NEVER share your password with anyone — including IT staff.
     IT will never ask for your password.
   - Do not write passwords on sticky notes or store them in plain text files.
   - Use the company-approved password manager for storing complex passwords.
   - Always log out of shared or public computers after use.
   - Report any suspicious login activity or unauthorized access immediately.
   - Passwords must be unique per system — do not reuse the same password
     across corporate and personal accounts.
