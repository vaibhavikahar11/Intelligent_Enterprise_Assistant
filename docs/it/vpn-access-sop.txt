1. PURPOSE
----------
This SOP governs the setup, usage, and security requirements for Virtual Private
Network (VPN) access at Acme Corporation. VPN is the approved method for securely
accessing internal company resources — including file servers, internal applications,
and development environments — from outside the corporate office network.

All employees, contractors, and authorized users who require remote access to
internal systems MUST use the company-approved VPN solution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. VPN CLIENT — GLOBALPROTECT
-------------------------------
   - Approved VPN Solution: GlobalProtect by Palo Alto Networks.
   - Download the GlobalProtect VPN client from the company's internal
     software distribution portal:
     URL: https://software.company.com
   - Log in to the software portal using your corporate credentials to
     access the download section.

   INSTALLATION STEPS:
   Step 1: Visit https://software.company.com and log in.
   Step 2: Navigate to "VPN & Remote Access" in the software catalog.
   Step 3: Download the GlobalProtect installer for your operating system
           (Windows 10/11, macOS, or Linux).
   Step 4: Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
   Step 5: Once installed, open GlobalProtect from the system tray or
           Applications folder.
   Step 6: When prompted for the VPN Gateway address, enter:
           vpn.company.com
   Step 7: Complete the MFA verification to finalize the first connection.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. VPN SERVER & CONNECTION DETAILS
------------------------------------
   - VPN Gateway Address: vpn.company.com
   - Authentication: Corporate Active Directory credentials
     (same username and password used for email/Windows login).
   - Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): REQUIRED for all VPN connections.
     Approve the MFA push notification on Microsoft Authenticator or
     enter the time-based OTP when prompted.
   - Protocol: IPSec/SSL (automatically negotiated by GlobalProtect).
   - DNS: VPN automatically routes internal DNS queries when connected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. WHEN IS VPN REQUIRED?
--------------------------
   VPN access is MANDATORY in the following situations:
   a) Accessing any internal company application from outside the office
      (e.g., ERP, HRMS, source code repositories, internal dashboards).
   b) Accessing file shares on internal servers or NAS devices.
   c) Using Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) to connect to office workstations.
   d) Accessing databases or API endpoints hosted on the internal network.
   e) Any remote administrative activity on company infrastructure.

   NOTE: Public-facing company websites, the Microsoft 365 suite (Outlook,
   Teams, SharePoint Online), and external SaaS tools do NOT require VPN
   as they are accessible over the internet directly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. REQUESTING VPN ACCESS
--------------------------
   - VPN access is not automatically granted to all employees.
   - Employees who require VPN access must submit a formal IT ticket:
     Helpdesk Portal: https://helpdesk.company.com
   - In the ticket, select category: "Access Request" → "VPN Access."
   - Provide justification for VPN access and specify required internal resources.
   - The request must be endorsed by the direct manager (manager approval
     required before IT provisioning).
   - Standard VPN access is approved and provisioned within 2 (two)
     BUSINESS DAYS of receiving a manager-approved request.
   - For urgent VPN requirements, mark the ticket as "High Priority" and
     contact the IT helpdesk directly at Ext. 1234.

6. VPN SECURITY POLICIES
--------------------------

   6.1 SPLIT TUNNELING — DISABLED
      - Split tunneling is DISABLED on the corporate VPN.
      - This means ALL internet traffic — including personal browsing —
        is routed through the company VPN tunnel when connected.
      - This is a deliberate security measure to prevent data exfiltration
        and ensure that all traffic is subject to company security controls.
      - As a consequence, general internet browsing may be slower while
        on VPN. Employees should disconnect from VPN if they do not
        require internal access at that moment, rather than browsing
        personal content over the corporate VPN.

   6.2 SESSION TIMEOUT
      - VPN sessions will automatically DISCONNECT after 8 (eight) hours
        of IDLE activity (no data transmission).
      - If the session times out, simply reconnect via the GlobalProtect client.
      - Active sessions with continuous data activity will not be interrupted.
      - Sessions are forcibly terminated at midnight and must be manually
        re-established each working day.

   6.3 ACCEPTABLE USE
      - The VPN must be used ONLY for legitimate company business purposes.
      - Do NOT use the corporate VPN for:
        * Personal streaming services (Netflix, YouTube, etc.)
        * Torrenting or P2P file sharing
        * Accessing personal social media for prolonged periods
        * Circumventing geo-restrictions on non-business sites
        * Any illegal or policy-violating activity
      - Using VPN resources for personal browsing degrades performance
        for all users and may result in VPN access revocation.

   6.4 VPN MONITORING & LOGGING
      - All VPN connection activity and data flows are LOGGED and MONITORED
        by the IT Security team for security compliance purposes.
      - Logs include: connection timestamps, source IP addresses, destination
        internal resources, and data volume transferred.
      - These logs are retained for a minimum of 12 months as per the
        company's IT Security Policy and applicable regulations.
      - Anomalous activity (e.g., connections at unusual hours, large data
        transfers) will trigger automated security alerts and may result
        in account suspension pending investigation.
      - Employees have no expectation of privacy when using corporate VPN.

7. TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON VPN ISSUES
--------------------------------------

   Issue: GlobalProtect fails to connect.
   Fix:   Ensure you have an active internet connection. Check that the
          gateway address is vpn.company.com. Verify your password has
          not expired at https://identity.company.com.

   Issue: MFA push notification not received.
   Fix:   Check phone connectivity and the Microsoft Authenticator app.
          Use the OTP code method as an alternative. Re-enroll MFA if needed.

   Issue: Connected to VPN but cannot access internal resources.
   Fix:   Disconnect and reconnect. Verify that your VPN access request
          covers the specific resource. Raise an IT ticket if the issue persists.

   Issue: VPN performance is very slow.
   Fix:   Disconnect and reconnect. Try connecting from a different network.
          Report persistent slow VPN performance to the helpdesk.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. VPN ACCESS REVOCATION
--------------------------
   VPN access will be automatically revoked in the following scenarios:
   - Employee separation (resignation, termination, or end of contract).
   - Extended leave of absence exceeding 90 days.
   - Security policy violation detected via VPN monitoring logs.
   - Manager-requested revocation for any business reason.
